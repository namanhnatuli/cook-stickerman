### **EVALUATION FRAMEWORK (PLATFORM POLICY VS. EDITORIAL INTEGRITY):**

#### **A. Official YouTube Platform Policies (Community, Monetization & Advertiser-Friendly Guidelines):**
YouTube evaluates content based on **context, graphic severity, intent, and authentic human value across the entire package** (video, title, thumbnail, description, tags), not isolated keyword triggers:
1. **Context-Based Culinary Standard:** Ordinary references to knives, blades, cutting, oven heat, and minor kitchen mishaps in an educational or culinary context are standard practice and evaluated by intent and context. Do not flag benign culinary tools or accurate baking terminology as policy violations.
2. **Violence, Blood & Injury Focus:** Content is penalized when it graphically or sensationally showcases gore, open wounds, bodily fluids, physical violence, or severe suffering. Historical accidents must be presented with neutral documentary dignity.
3. **Self-Harm & Sensitive History:** Promotion, glorification, instructions, imitable details, or graphic depictions of self-harm are prohibited or high-risk. Fleeting, non-graphic historical or documentary references (such as wartime famine, poverty food origins, or industrial accidents) must be evaluated by context, focus, detail, title, thumbnail, and visuals rather than isolated keywords.
4. **Sensational Deception & Medical Misinformation:** Exaggerating food toxicity, claiming common ingredients are lethal poisons, or fabricating medical emergencies triggers scrutiny. YouTube's spam and deceptive metadata policies strictly penalize malicious clickbait or packaging that fundamentally misrepresents video content.
5. **Channel Authenticity & Anti-Repetitive Content Policy ([answer/1311392](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)):**
   - In accordance with YouTube Channel Monetization Policies on "reused content" and "repetitive content", channels must provide significant original commentary, pedagogical transformation, and authentic educational value. Generic or repetitive mass-produced templates may make the channel ineligible for monetization.
   - *Note on Educational Formats:* YouTube reviews monetization eligibility at the channel level. Using consistent branding, recurrent graphics, and a predictable pedagogical structure across an educational explainer series is fully permissible provided individual episodes deliver significant original commentary, distinctive educational value, and substantively different core subject matter.
   - **Audit Checks:**
     - *Narrative Transformation:* Original scientific and culinary explanations rather than automated scraping, robotic encyclopedic summaries, or unmodified regurgitation of third-party texts.
     - *Creator Perspective & Voice:* Distinctive pedagogical thesis, engaging storytelling, and active educational framing rather than generic automated filler.
     - *Structural & Content Diversity:* Dynamic pacing, diverse narrative lenses (science, history, disaster, texture), and custom visual demonstrations ensuring each episode stands on its own distinct merits.

#### **B. Cook Stickerman Epistemic Integrity Standard (Channel Editorial Codex):**
Beyond YouTube's baseline deception rules, Cook Stickerman enforces rigorous epistemic fidelity to ensure packaging never misleads viewers on the scientific or historical certainty of claims:
- `[CONFIRMED]` claims may be stated factually in title/thumbnail/description.
- `[DISPUTED]` claims must retain qualified framing (e.g., competing theories, contested origins).
- `[POPULAR MYTH]` claims must remain explicitly myth/legend-framed.
- Asserting a disputed hypothesis or popular myth as established factual truth in packaging (e.g., claiming *"THE TRUE INVENTOR"* on a contested origin) violates channel epistemic standards.
- Every factual or epistemically qualified packaging statement must map directly to an approved Claim ID in the Final Production Claim Ledger.
