### **EVALUATION & REVIEW MATRIX (THE 6 AUDIT PILLARS):**

Evaluate the draft script against these 6 viral benchmarks. For each benchmark, rate it **[Pass / Needs Work / Fail]** and provide specific, actionable critique:

1. **The First 15 Seconds (Hook & Immediate Stakes):**
   - Did it dive straight into a curiosity-inducing fact or shocking anomaly?
   - Is there any fluff, preamble, or generic greeting that needs to be killed immediately?
2. **Scientific Depth vs. Accessibility (The Science Test):**
   - Are the chemical/culinary explanations specific (e.g., precise reactions, temperatures, gluten behavior, aeration mechanisms) rather than generic baking advice?
   - Is the science easy to visualize, or does it sound like a dry textbook?
3. **The "Myth/Lore/Twist" Factor (Story Depth):**
   - Does each cake/item contain an interesting narrative angle (wartime rationing, corporate marketing scheme, accidental discovery, cultural clash)?
   - Are there missing historical anecdotes that should be injected?
4. **Pacing & Sensory Rhythm (Audio Flow):**
   - Are the sentences punchy and varied in length to facilitate natural voiceover breathing and cadence?
   - Does it use rich, tactile sensory adjectives (*brittle, velvety, molten, pillowy, dense, shatter*) instead of bland words (*tasty, good, sweet*)?
5. **Visual Viability & Cue Density (Director's Eye):**
   - Are there sufficient and descriptive `[Visual Cue]` tags?
   - Would an editor/animator clearly know what on-screen graphic, sticker animation, diagram, or archival clip to display at any given second?
6. **Retention Traps & Fluff Detection:**
   - Identify redundant explanations, filler words, repetitive transition phrases (e.g., constantly repeating "next up we have..."), or pacing drag.
