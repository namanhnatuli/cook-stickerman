# EDITORIAL WRITING CODEX & VOICE GUIDELINES FOR YOUTUBE EXPLAINERS
*Standardized rules for tone, sentence length, narrative cadence, transitions, vocabulary, compliance, and structural pacing for all channel scripts.*

---

## ⚡ THE 6 NON-NEGOTIABLE CORE LAWS (6 LUẬT BẮT BUỘC)
*Every script written for this channel must strictly obey these 6 overarching operational mandates:*

1. **Zero Hallucination & Explicit Verification Gaps:**
   - Never fabricate or invent dates, names, chemical mechanisms, numbers, or anecdotes.
   - If any claim, detail, or statistic is uncertain, leave it blank and explicitly flag it with **`[NEEDS VERIFICATION]` / `[CẦN KIỂM CHỨNG]`**. Never bluff or invent facts.

2. **The 3-Point Soft Engagement Rule (3 Lời Kêu Gọi Tương Tác Kín Đáo):**
   - Every script must organically distribute **exactly 3 soft calls-to-action (CTAs)** across the video:
     - **Early CTA (First 20%):** A light diagnostic question or subtle curiosity hook (*"If you've ever had this happen in your kitchen, tell us in the comments..."*).
     - **Mid-Roll CTA (Around 50%):** A texture debate or baking opinion prompt embedded inside a causal comparison (*"Let us know down below: do you lean toward a fudgy bite or an airy crumb?"*).
     - **Outro CTA (Final 10%):** A clean community suggestion prompt for upcoming episodes (*"What baking science or dessert mystery should we break down next? Let us know in the comments. Thanks for watching."*).
   - All 3 must feel natural, smooth, conversational, and completely unforced—never aggressive or salesy.

3. **Concrete Specificity Mandate (Anti-Vagueness):**
   - Strictly prohibit vague, generic writing (*"long ago"*, *"a lot of sugar"*, *"very high heat"*, *"many people"*).
   - **Always provide:**
     - **Exact Numbers & Ratios:** Specific temperatures (e.g., *200°C / 400°F*), exact years/centuries (*1875*, *1927*), precise ratios (*1:1 fat-to-flour*, *60% marbling*), or specific percentages.
     - **Spatial & Temporal Grounding:** Specific geography, towns, war eras, depression-era rationings, royal kitchens, or industrial factories.
     - **Tangible Sensory Emotion:** Exact tactile mouthfeel and physical reactions (*tight crumb, shatter, glassy snap, velvety tongue-coat*).

4. **Policy Compliance & Euphemistic Reframing (An Toàn Chính Sách):**
   - Avoid sensitive, violent, or policy-triggering keywords that risk demonetization or age-restrictions on YouTube:
     - Knife / Scissors / Blade $\rightarrow$ **"Tool" / "Cutting instrument"** (*công cụ, lưỡi cắt*).
     - Blood $\rightarrow$ **"Red streaks" / "Deep crimson pigment"** (*vệt đỏ, sắc tố đỏ*).
     - Die / Killed / Suicide $\rightarrow$ **"Passed away" / "Lost their life"** (*qua đời*).
   - Any sensitive historical tragedy, famine, or accident must be narrated through a calm, neutral, documentary lens without graphic sensationalism.

5. **AI / CGI Graphic Disclosure:**
   - Whenever a visual cue or narration utilizes an AI-generated mock-up, conceptual composite, or 3D synthetic simulation, the script must explicitly include an on-screen notice or subtle voiceover disclaimer stating that it is an **illustrative simulation / concept render** (*hình ảnh minh hoạ*).

6. **Mathematical Word-Budget Tolerance (Dung Sai Số Từ ±10%):**
   - The requested word budget is allowed an official operating tolerance of **±10%** to accommodate natural storytelling rhythm, breath pauses, and pedagogical clarity.

---

## I. CORE PHILOSOPHY & PERSONA

1. **The Narrator Persona (The Observant Specialist):**
   - Sound like a calm, deeply knowledgeable, and observant specialist.
   - Never sound like an over-caffeinated YouTuber, a pushy commercial pitchman, or a dry academic lecturer.
   - Rely strictly on verified facts, physical/chemical mechanisms, and grounded historical reality. Let the inherent fascination of the facts carry the script.

2. **Perspective & Address (Point of View):**
   - **Third-Person Objective (85–90% of narration):** The subject of each sentence should almost always be the food itself, the physical crumb, the reaction mechanism, or the historical actor (*"The cake...", "Heat moves upward...", "Bakers soon realized..."*).
   - **Direct Second-Person ("You"):** Use *"you"* selectively when placing the viewer in the tactile sensory moment, a physical kitchen technique, or a diagnostic baking decision (*"When you cut across the grain...", "If you bite in, the sugar shell shatters first"*).
   - **Zero Self-Reference in Body ("I / Me" Banned):** Never use *"I"*, *"me"*, or *"my"* in the body. The narrator steps back entirely to keep the spotlight on the knowledge.
   - **First-Person Plural ("We / Us"):** Reserved exclusively for the outro when inviting community input (*"Let us know in the comments what we should explain next"*).

## II. SENTENCE CADENCE & NARRATIVE RHYTHM

### 1. Sentence Length Spectrum
High retention is driven by continuous contrast between punchy short statements and medium explanatory clauses, avoiding monotone pacing:

- **Target Average Length:** **14 – 18 words per sentence** (~5–7 seconds of voiceover).
- **Short Leverage Sentences ("Punch Sentences": 3 – 7 words):** Constitute ~20% of the script. Used to anchor a definition, crystallize a mechanism, or create dramatic punctuation:
  - *Archetypes:* *"That's it."*, *"Heat dulls it fast."*, *"The brittle crack is the feature."*, *"It defies physics."*, *"One is about freshness."*, *"The other is about background depth."*
- **Mechanism Explanations (Mechanical Sentences: 18 – 25 words):** Used to trace an unbroken causal chain:
  - *Standard Formula:* `[Condition / Action]` + `[Physical/Chemical Mechanism]` + `[Visible Crumb / Texture Outcome]`.
  - *Example:* *"As the batter heats in the oven, trapped water converts into expanding steam, forcing alternating fat layers apart while coagulating proteins set the final crumb structure."*
- **Hard Word Cap:** Never exceed **28 words in a single sentence**. If a sentence reaches 30 words, split it immediately with a period to provide natural voiceover breathing room.

### 2. Micro-Block Rhythm (Item Pacing)
Each individual item breakdown (spanning 45 to 75 seconds / ~100–160 words) follows a 3-beat movement:
- **Beat 1 (Entry - 10s):** Direct identity statement + Paradox or structural anomaly.
- **Beat 2 (Dissection - 30–50s):** The physical/chemical causal chain, ingredient ratio, or confirmed historical pivot.
- **Beat 3 (Punctuation - 10–15s):** A sharp sensory payoff, modern culinary consequence, or critical failure point.

## III. ZERO-FLUFF HOOK PATTERNS

### 1. Overall Video Hook (First 15 Seconds)
- **Strict Bans:** Zero greetings (*"Hey guys, welcome back"*), zero channel intros, zero pre-roll subscription begs.
- **High-Retention Opening Formulas:**
  - *The Category Rule Inversion:*
    `[Category items] do not all behave the same way. Some [A], some [B], and some only work when [C]. Once you understand that, everything else makes sense.`
  - *The Historical / Chemical Anomaly:*
    `The most famous [item] wasn't always [familiar characteristic]. Before [Year / Breakthrough], it couldn't exist because the chemistry wasn't there yet.`
  - *The Physical Tension:*
    `[Item] has the simplest ingredient list in pastry, but the most unforgiving technique in baking.`

### 2. Item-Level Openings (Entry Hooks)
Always state the **Item Name as the beat marker**, then deploy one of 3 entry angles:
1. **Identity-First (Structural Definition):**
   - `[Item Name] is [core structural format] with [defining physical trait].`
   - *Example:* *"Creme Brulee is a baked custard with a hard sugar crust you are supposed to crack."*
2. **Lore-First (Confirmed Historical Twist):**
   - `[Item Name]. The [signature feature] wasn't always from [popular assumption]. It was a [verified accident / event].`
   - *Example:* *"Red velvet cake. The red color wasn't always from food dye. It was a chemical accident."*
3. **Failure-First (Technical Tolerance):**
   - `[Item Name]. One of the most direct ways to bake, and the least forgiving in terms of [technique / temperature].`
   - *Example:* *"Pound cake has the simplest ingredient list but the most unforgiving technique."*

## IV. SEAMLESS TRANSITIONS & STRUCTURAL CONNECTIVITY

Never use artificial, mechanical YouTube transitions (*"Next up on our list is..."*, *"Moving on to the next cake..."*, *"Another interesting dessert is..."*).

Use the 4 native explainer transition techniques:

### 1. The Boundary Shift
Transitioning by altering a single structural variable:
- *Formula:* *"Move the ratio even slightly, and you land in a completely different dessert."*
- *Formula:* *"Where [Item A] builds quietly in the background, [Item B] announces itself immediately."*

### 2. The Hard Jump (Noun-Drop)
Finish the previous item on a firm, downward-inflected punch sentence $\rightarrow$ Pause 0.5s $\rightarrow$ State the next item name clearly with vocal emphasis:
- *Example:* *"...that is how it went from wartime poverty bread to an $8 cafe staple. [0.5s Pause] **Chiffon cake**..."*

### 3. Causal Internal Linkers
Within an item, advance ideas through logical physical progression rather than filler conjunctions:
- *"Which is why..."*
- *"The trade-off is..."*
- *"Instead of [A], [B] happens..."*
- *"That intensity is also the risk."*

---

## V. CURIOSITY SEEDING & QUESTION FRAMING

Questions must never be generic filler. They serve as **diagnostic probes** that articulate the exact doubt or intuition the viewer secretly harbors:

1. **The Diagnostic Probe (Validating Hidden Doubts):**
   - *Formula:* *"If you have ever wondered why [common baking anomaly occurs], the answer comes down to [physical mechanism]."*
   - *Formula:* *"Many home bakers have wondered at some point whether [ingredient X] actually does anything. It does, but not in the way most people think."*
2. **The Intuitive Paradox:**
   - *Formula:* *"How does a sponge cake hold nearly three times its weight in liquid without dissolving into mush? It defies physics."*
3. **The Sensory Outro Prompt (Comment Seeding):**
   - Never ask lazy open questions (*"What did you think of the video?"*).
   - Force an opinion on **texture trade-offs** or **culinary myths**:
   - *Texture Preference:* *"Do you prefer your brownies dense and fudgy with a crackly top, or cakey and light? Let us know in the comments."*
   - *Next Topic Direction:* *"What baking technique or dessert category should we break down next? Thanks for watching."*

## VI. LEXICON: ESSENTIAL POWER WORDS VS. THE BLACKLIST

### 1. The Power Lexicon (Precision & Sensory Immersion)

#### A. Physical & Chemical Precision
| Term | Precise Editorial Application |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Coagulate / Coagulation** | Protein denaturation and setting under heat (eggs, dairy proteins). |
| **Aerate / Aeration** | Mechanically incorporating and stabilizing micro-air bubbles in fat or foam. |
| **Gluten inhibition / development**| Controlling elastic flour protein networks to balance chewiness vs. tenderness. |
| **Emulsify / Emulsification** | Suspending incompatible fat and water molecules without breaking. |
| **Gelatinize / Gelatinization** | Starch granules absorbing liquid, swelling, and setting upon heating. |
| **Steam lift / Expansion** | Internal water flashing to steam to inflate hollow cavities or expand batter. |
| **Maillard reaction / Caramelization** | High-heat browning of amino acids and sugars producing complex flavor notes. |

#### B. Sensory & Tactile Descriptors
| Descriptor | Targeted Crumb / Mouthfeel Sensation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Brittle crack / Glassy shell** | Rigid crystalline snap that shatters under teeth (sugar crusts, tuiles). |
| **Pillowy / Springy** | High-elasticity rebound when pressed; airy softness (chiffon, steamed sponge). |
| **Dense and chewy / Fudgy** | Low-aeration, moisture-heavy resistance with rich mouth-coating fat. |
| **Molten / Oozing** | Viscous, warm, fluid interior that has intentionally not fully coagulated. |
| **Velvety crumb / Tight crumb**| Fine, uniform cell structure with zero harsh graininess. |
| **Delicate wobble** | Unstable, soft gel structure that holds shape on a plate but quivers gently. |
| **Shatter** | Thin, laminated multi-layer crusts fracturing instantly into delicate flakes. |

### 2. The Blacklist & Policy Replacements (Từ Cấm & Từ Thay Thế An Toàn)

| Forbidden Word / Phrase | Why It Is Banned | Approved Replacement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Delicious / Tasty / Yummy"** | Subjective, lazy, and conveys zero texture or flavor physics. | Describe flavor notes & mouthfeel: *buttery, rich, sharp, floral, bittersweet, nutty*. |
| **"Next up / Moving on to..."** | Amateur, mechanical transition that ruptures narrative momentum. | Use **The Boundary Shift** or **The Hard Jump**. |
| **"As you probably know..."** | Presumptuous, condescending, and alienates novice viewers. | Reframe objectively: *"Most people assume X, but the reality is..."* |
| **"Mind-blowing / Shocking"** | Empty hype and cheap clickbait. | State the factual contradiction calmly; let the facts do the work. |
| **"In this video, I will show you..."**| Slows the opening and wastes the critical 15-second retention window. | Cut entirely. Start directly with the core hook. |
| **Knife / Blade / Scissors (Dao, Kéo)** | Triggers YouTube safety/violence algorithmic flags. | **"Tool"**, **"Cutting instrument"**, **"Baker's blade"** (*công cụ, dụng cụ cắt*). |
| **Blood (Máu)** | Triggers content sensitivity and demonetization filters. | **"Red streaks"**, **"Crimson pigment"** (*vệt đỏ, sắc tố đỏ*). |
| **Killed / Suicide / Die (Chết, Tự tử)** | High-risk demonetization triggers. | **"Passed away"**, **"Lost their life"** (*qua đời*). |
| **Begging for likes/subscribes in the intro** | Destroys early retention graph. | Place a single, crisp 5-second call-to-action in the outro only. |

## VII. PRE-EXPORT SCRIPT AUDIT CHECKLIST

Before any script is approved for voiceover recording, verify these 10 editorial gates:
1. [ ] **Opening Hook:** Zero greetings; opens immediately on a verified tension, category rule, or chemical anomaly within 15 seconds.
2. [ ] **Fact Verification:** Zero invented facts. Any unverified detail is explicitly tagged `[NEEDS VERIFICATION]` / `[CẦN KIỂM CHỨNG]`.
3. [ ] **3-Point Soft CTAs:** Exactly 3 natural, unforced CTAs placed at early (~20%), mid (~50%), and outro (~90%).
4. [ ] **Specificity Check:** Contains exact numbers, temperatures, ratios, years, and specific geography. Zero vague expressions.
5. [ ] **Policy Safety:** Zero sensitive words (no knife/blood/suicide terms; replaced with *tools*, *crimson streaks*, *passed away*).
6. [ ] **AI Asset Disclosure:** If AI/CGI visuals are used, a clear disclaimer label or spoken line marks them as illustrative concept renders.
7. [ ] **Word Budget Tolerance:** Total words fall strictly within ±10% of the calculated duration target.
8. [ ] **Sentence Length:** No single sentence exceeds 28 words; punch sentences (3–7 words) are actively interspersed.
9. [ ] **Causal Rigor:** Every single item communicates its chain: `[Technique/Ratio]` → `[Mechanism]` → `[Visible Result]`.
10. [ ] **Perspective Integrity:** Strict third-person objective in body; zero instances of *"I"*, *"me"*, or *"my"*.
